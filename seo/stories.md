Storytelling:
Start the narrative in the middle of the action (e.g., "Three weeks ago, our inbox caught fire...").
Use specific names (or pseudonyms) and concrete details rather than generic labels like "A customer."
Dedicate 70% of the story to the failure, obstacle, or frustration before introducing the successful solution.
Cap micro-anecdotes at 100 words max.
Tie the story's "moral" directly to the current H2/H3 topic being discussed.
Focus on the internal feeling of the problem, not just the external logistics.
