Voice:
Adopt the "Coffee Shop" rule: Write exactly as you would explain your product to a smart friend who works in a totally different field.
Ban all corporate jargon universally (strike: leverage, synergy, bandwidth, paradigm shift, holistic).
Use active voice exclusively ("Our team fixed the bug" instead of "The bug was fixed by our team").
Read every draft out loud; delete any sentence where you naturally run out of breath.
Use sensory, concrete nouns instead of abstract business concepts.
End sections with a direct question to the reader to maintain conversational momentum.

Persona:
Write in the first person: Use "I" and "me" to share personal perspectives.
Address the reader directly: Use "you" to create a one-on-one conversation.
Use sentence fragments: Use short bursts for emphasis.
Start sentences with conjunctions: Use "But," "And," or "Because" at the start of sentences.
Adopt a "venting" tone: Write as if explaining a concept to a friend over coffee or at a bar.
Admit fallibility: Share a mistake, a "facepalm" moment, or a time you were wrong about the topic.
