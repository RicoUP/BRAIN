Vocabulary & AI Detection Removal
Ban the phrases: "In today's fast-paced world," "In the digital landscape," "When it comes to," and "A testament to."
Eliminate "Conclusion" clichés: Never use "In conclusion," "To summarize," or "Ultimately."
Remove robotic transitions: Strike "Moreover," "Furthermore," "Additionally," and "On the other hand."
Delete AI buzzwords: "Delve," "Tapestry," "Harness," "Pivotal," "Synergy," "Robust," and "Comprehensive guide."
Break the "Rule of Three": Avoid grouping adjectives or benefits into clean triplets.
Avoid uniform sentence length: Mix 3-word sentences with 25-word sentences.
Use contractions exclusively: Use "don't," "can't," and "you're" instead of "do not," "cannot," or "you are."
