Editing & Quality Control:
Perform the "Read Aloud" test: Rewrite any sentence that makes you stumble or run out of breath.
Apply the "First Paragraph Kill" rule: Delete the entire introductory paragraph and start at the second.
Replace generalizations with "Dave": Change "many users" to a specific fictional person or scenario.
Remove "fluff" qualifiers: Delete words like "very," "really," "just," and "perhaps."
Check the "Cringe" factor: If a sentence feels like a corporate brochure, delete it.
